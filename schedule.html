Conference Schedule Timeline

Sunday, October 18th, 2026 | Dream Palace, Paroor

08:30 AM - 09:30 AM CHECK-IN

Delegate Reception & Registration Pass Verification

Delegate check-in, official TEENSPACE kit distribution, seating allocation, and morning refreshment.

09:30 AM - 10:30 AM KEYNOTE

Inaugural Session & Spiritual Orientation

Opening recitation, welcome ceremony by Wisdom Students Thrissur District Leaders, and keynote address.

Speaker: Rauof Al Hikami
10:30 AM - 11:45 AM SESSION 1

Building Strong Islamic Character & Moral Ethics

Interactive discussion on Islamic values, personal discipline, respect for parents and teachers, and moral integrity.

Speaker: Shafeeq Swalahi
11:45 AM - 01:00 PM SESSION 2

Quran & Sunnah Guidance in Student Life

Practical insights on applying Quranic principles, daily supplications, and Sunnah practices during high school years.

Speaker: Safwan Barami
01:00 PM - 02:00 PM INTERMISSION

Dhuhr Congregational Prayer & Lunch Break

Congregational Dhuhr prayer led at the venue followed by delegate lunch and networking.

02:00 PM - 03:15 PM SESSION 3

Navigating Contemporary Youth Challenges & Faith

Addressing modern ideological challenges, peer pressure, identity, and safeguarding Islamic belief in a fast-changing world.

Speaker: Mujahid Balussery
03:15 PM - 04:30 PM WORKSHOP

Career Guidance, Anti-Drug Awareness & Digital Detox

Strategic higher secondary stream choices, entrance exam orientation, substance abuse prevention, and healthy screen habits.

Speaker: Shamjas K Abbas
04:30 PM - 05:00 PM VALEDICTORY

Valedictory Ceremony & Concluding Supplication

Summary of conference resolutions, delegate certificate announcements, and concluding Dua.
